Choose the CAN connection

Connect a supported SocketCAN interface. Set its bitrate from the vehicle specification. Discovery reads metadata and leaves your network settings unchanged.

Passive signals and provenance

Only use a mapping verified against your ECU specification or recording. There are no guessed VW or race-car broadcast profiles. The built-in obd_mode01 profile observes existing OBD responses and requires a response ID.

Bit order: little = Intel LSB first; big = network MSB first. Big is not DBC Motorola sawtooth numbering. See the installed signal reference before entering a mapping.
